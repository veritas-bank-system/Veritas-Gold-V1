PURPOSE
-------
Track decisions and user-visible changes for the Account Overview feature while preserving the current website. Prepare the showcase for a VPS deployment path and playtest the real UI. A VPS-hosted demo is not a production-authorized central-bank settlement service.

DECISIONS
---------
1. Replace consumer/corporate-card language with settlement account and liquidity operations appropriate to a central-bank operator.
   Reason: the prior page presented “Total Spending Power” and “Corporate Card”, obscuring the difference between settled funds and overdraft credit.

2. Show settled balance and approved overdraft/credit facility as separate amounts. Show a combined indicative ceiling only as settled balance plus approved credit, and explicitly state it is not spendable cash and excludes unavailable reservations/holds.
   Reason: current demo accounts have €0 settled balance and €1,000/€500 overdrafts; reserved/blocked/pending amounts are not part of the account API.

3. Display only account API facts: account ID, currency, settled balance, daily transfer limit, accumulated debit, remaining limit, status, owner/custodian reference and update timestamp. Treat demo genesis timestamp as “Demo seed record.”
   Reason: do not fabricate account type, legal entity names, RTGS positions, pending settlement or bank-wide liquidity.

4. Replace hardcoded quick-pay recipient buttons with a sandbox transfer form restricted to active account IDs returned by the API. Validate source/destination differ, positive amount, indicative account headroom and remaining daily limit. State that the demo action writes to in-memory ledger, without approval or real rail.
   Reason: Swiss Vault quick-pay used an account ID absent from the API; tests should not mislead or send a fake external payment.

5. Preserve the dark/red design, account route, and existing sandbox transfer API. Keep the work scoped to the overview screen and existing VPS launcher rather than changing unrelated modules.
   Reason: user requested new features while maintaining current functionality and look.

6. Treat VPS readiness as a reproducible demo build/run path. No external deployment is performed and no claim of live central-bank / RTGS/SWIFT production readiness is made.
   Reason: current service has seeded in-memory records, no production authentication, persistence, partner integration, external approval, or VPS credentials.

---------------------

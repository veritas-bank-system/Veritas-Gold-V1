- **Purpose**: Corporate governance and separation of duties for transactions $> \text{€100,000}$.

- **Purpose**: Real-time physical bullion verification directly from Zurich vaults.
- **Features**: Weight sensor telemetry, ultrasonic acoustic density scan, temperature/humidity monitors, and Merkle root Proof-of-Reserve.

### 🤖 Module 5: Programmable Liquidity Sweeping Rules
- **Purpose**: Automated corporate cash management and yield optimization.
- **Features**: Auto-sweeping idle cash balances above threshold into tokenized US Treasuries (`USTB`) or Gold (`GOLD`) on configurable schedules.
